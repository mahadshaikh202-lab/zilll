OUR STORY

Every Great Brand Begins With A Dream.

ZYVEL was founded by Mahad Shaikh, a young entrepreneur with a simple but powerful vision to create a fragrance brand that represents elegance, confidence and unforgettable memories.

At just 19 years old, Mahad started ZYVEL with the dream of building more than a perfume brand. His goal is to create fragrances that people wear with pride and remember for years to come.

Every step of ZYVEL is driven by passion, creativity and the belief that true luxury should be experienced by everyone.

Today, ZYVEL is only the beginning. Tomorrow, the vision is to take this brand from Pakistan to the world.
